Welcome to the Lobster Bowl

The Lobster Bowl may be a restaurant, but to us, it is an experience!
Founded by Jebediah Projansky using his love of seafood, the Lobster Bowl began as an mere idea and grew into a larger, more delicious idea. And, with our locally sourced fish, shrimp, and lobster renowned by guests everywhere, it seems that idea was a success!
